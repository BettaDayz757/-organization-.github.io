# BettaDayZ - PBBG Crime & Dungeon Game 🎮

**Crime Dungeon Style With Life Simulation**

A fully-featured persistent browser-based game (PBBG) that combines crime simulation, dungeon exploration, and life management mechanics. Build your criminal empire while exploring dangerous dungeons!

## 🎯 Play Now

Visit: [https://bettadayz757.github.io/-organization-.github.io/](https://bettadayz757.github.io/-organization-.github.io/)

## 🎮 Game Features

### Core Gameplay
- **Crime System**: 6 different criminal activities from pickpocketing to bank heists
- **Dungeon Exploration**: 3 difficulty levels with combat and treasure hunting
- **Life Simulation**: Work, train, rest, and socialize to build your character
- **Equipment Shop**: Purchase weapons and armor to increase your power
- **Inventory Management**: Collect, equip, and sell items

### Character Development
- **Dynamic Stats**: Health, Energy, Money, Reputation, Strength, Agility, Intelligence
- **XP & Leveling**: Gain experience and level up to unlock new abilities
- **Skill Training**: Train individual stats to customize your character
- **Equipment System**: Weapons and armor that affect combat effectiveness

### Game Progression
- **Risk/Reward System**: Higher-risk crimes offer greater rewards
- **Combat Mechanics**: Strength, agility, and equipment determine success
- **Random Events**: Dynamic loot drops and crime outcomes
- **Auto-Save**: Progress saved automatically every minute

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. **Start Your Journey**: Begin as a low-level criminal with basic stats
2. **Earn Money**: Work honest jobs or commit crimes for quick cash
3. **Train Skills**: Invest in training to increase your stats
4. **Buy Equipment**: Visit the shop to purchase weapons and armor
5. **Explore Dungeons**: Fight enemies and discover treasure
6. **Level Up**: Gain XP through all activities to increase your power

## 📊 Game Mechanics

### Stats Explained
- **Health**: Your life points - reaching 0 causes death and money loss
- **Energy**: Required for most actions - restore by resting
- **Money**: Currency for buying items and training
- **Reputation**: Increases with socializing, decreases with crime
- **Strength**: Increases combat power in dungeons
- **Agility**: Improves crime success rate and combat
- **Intelligence**: Boosts work earnings and crime success

### Crime Activities
1. **Pickpocket** (Low Risk): $20-50, Energy: 10
2. **Mugging** (Medium Risk): $50-150, Energy: 15
3. **Burglary** (Medium Risk): $100-300, Energy: 20
4. **Carjacking** (High Risk): $200-500, Energy: 25
5. **Bank Heist** (Very High Risk): $1000-3000, Energy: 40
6. **Black Market** (High Risk): $500-1500, Energy: 30

### Dungeon Levels
- **Easy**: Low-level enemies, good for beginners
- **Medium**: Moderate challenge, better rewards
- **Hard**: Dangerous enemies, highest rewards

## 💾 Save System

- **Manual Save**: Save anytime using the Save Game button
- **LocalStorage**: All progress stored in your browser
